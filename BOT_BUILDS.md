# Survivor bots

Low, Medium and High use survivor-appropriate priority plans. Extra High and Ultra score their current inventory, survivor role, health/shields, crit, proc synergies and available pickups instead of following a fixed item list. Unknown custom items use their catalog tags; this is a heuristic, not a simulation of arbitrary mod scripts.

| Fixed plan | Main goals |
|---|---|
| On-hit | Syringes, daggers, missiles, lightning, shield support and later proc upgrades. |
| Critical | Glasses, Predatory Instincts, Harvester's Scythe, Shatterspleen and Laser Scope. |
| Skill caster | Backup Magazines, Kjaro's/Runald's Bands, Alien Head, Afterburner and Lysate Cell. |
| Bulwark | Block, armor, healing, health and nearby damage for sustained fights. |

Fixed plans select consistent ordinary/void routes per bot. They do not buy a void item that corrupts owned setup pieces, nor buy ordinary items already converted by a held void item. Caster plans retain ordinary bands instead of later destroying them with Singularity Band. Crit plans preserve their normal crit/bleed dependencies. Plasma Shrimp builds prioritize missing shield support; full-shield builds avoid health-only healing goals. Purity is not a default fixed goal alongside luck/crit/proc investments.

Ultra reads effective item counts and equipment in all accessible opponent slots. It considers up to three nearby opposing participants, with the last attacker prioritized after a death. Allies and self are excluded; FFA Chase mode focuses the same human opponents the bot hunts. Inventories are cached per master for half a second and shared by Ultra bots. Examples include multi-hit pressure against Safer Spaces, heavier hits against Repulsion Armor Plate, slows against mobility/sprint builds, armor reduction against armor, defence against crit/bands, and direct damage instead of relying on effects an opponent can cleanse. Choices remain within the exact host-created offer and respect all item bans/caps.

All tiers pursue and remain inside a ready KOTH capture area. Low has simpler aim/target selection; Medium keeps the earlier aim parameters and target scoring. High sees nearby opposing summons as combat targets. Extra High and Ultra add bounded projectile leading for known projectile survivors; hitscan skills use their normal aiming. Native utility/special press-release sequences are preserved, including after a target disappears.

Bot travel uses the native position-based path API. A bare waypoint has no hurtbox, so the target-based overload previously discarded it. The travel fallback has no attack skill and follows the native drivers in their original order. Registered bots opt into native cliff detection; extra ground probes run at most ten times per second per bot, with safe lateral alternatives. Validated graph jumps and in-flight utilities retain native handling. These checks prevent deliberate blind walking; they do not guarantee immunity to knockback, failed abilities or malformed navigation in custom maps.

Tactical and item-choice work share a round-robin, 2 ms soft budget per server physics update. Native AI/physics/pathfinding retain their own game costs. One indivisible physics query or native inventory callback can exceed the soft budget. Reward offers are rolled immediately on the host, then scored and consumed under this budget, including death choices without a surviving body. A bad offer stays banked with its original IDs/class while other offers can be considered. The 12-offer cap, catch-up accounting and host validation remain in force.

Extra High and Ultra add combat sidesteps, grounded jumps and sustained legal sprint requests. Aiming and firing skills can cancel sprinting; native SkillDef flags prevent restarting it while an attack is still active. Once recovery completes, a distinct release and re-press restores sprint without interrupting the skill. Native force-sprint skills retain their own handling. Unknown busy states and active vehicles are left alone. Combat jumps use actual jump power and prospective sprint speed to test overhead clearance, route and landing support; they never add air jumps or write velocity. Additional queries run only when the staggered jump cadence permits an attempt. Ground probes remain capped at 10 Hz and KOTH movement stays inside the capture area.

Ultra also sequences known survivor attacks. Commando follows secondary with burst fire, Artificer opens with freeze before charging a secondary and using its close-range special, and Bandit reserves his finisher for wounded targets. Huntress, Mercenary, REX, Acrid, MUL-T, CHEF and False Son have suitable attack orders; Captain, Engineer and Seeker use a conservative secondary/primary sequence. Native readiness and skill-driver filters decide whether a step is legal. Steps advance only on the body's actual server activation event and wait for recovery. Charged secondaries keep their button held before releasing normally. Loader, Railgunner and custom survivors retain native grapple, scope/reload and other state chains instead of guessed button combinations. Modded replacement states also retain native control. This is bounded tactical sequencing, not a complete simulation of every skill or loadout.

No AI or bot-item logic runs on joining clients. Clients receive the normal replicated masters, inventories and actions. Starting counts remain mode-specific; intelligence, decision interval and Chase are shared host settings. No optional mod or external AI service is required.
